welcome to i wrote today

A daily tracker for your writing journey.

Every day you write (or mean to), tap i wrote today (or i didn't, but i will tomorrow!) for a little word reward (a wordward, a re-word?).

After checking in for 3, 7, 14, 30, 60 and 180 days, you'll also receive some writing wisdom from authors who've walked the road.

Everything stays private, on your own phone.
