privacy

I Wrote Today · last updated 15 October 2026

I Wrote Today is a small daily writing tracker. It doesn't ask who you are, and it doesn't collect anything about you.

What stays on your phone

The days you check in, your streaks and any settings you choose are stored only on your own device. They are never sent to me or to anyone else, and there is no account to create. If you delete the app, that information is deleted with it.

What the app downloads

From time to time the app downloads the latest list of quotes and words from its own website, so new ones can appear without an app update. This request doesn't include any information about you or your writing.

Tracking, analytics and ads

None. The app has no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking tools.

Book links

Each quote links to the book on Bookshop.org, which opens in your browser. Those links may include an affiliate code, which means a small commission can go to the app's creator if you buy a book, at no extra cost to you. Once you are on Bookshop.org, their own privacy policy applies.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Questions

You can contact the developer through the contact details on the app's App Store or Google Play page.
